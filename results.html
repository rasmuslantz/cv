Engineering evidence

Signals I care about more than a green contribution square.

01

Real pull-request history

Feature work, release fixes, billing work, CI changes, storage refactors and product iterations are developed through actual repository changes and reviewable diffs.

02

Tests around risky behavior

Core calculations, persistence and edge cases are covered where a silent failure would damage user trust or data.

03

Release infrastructure

CI, signing, notarization and update trust are treated as engineering deliverables rather than manual steps hidden outside the repository.

04

Public architecture for private source

Private implementation does not need to mean unverifiable work. Product boundaries, architecture and design decisions can still be documented publicly.
