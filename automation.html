Reliability

The boring parts are usually the important parts.

01

Idempotency

A retry should not create duplicate work, duplicate messages or duplicate records.

02

Change detection

Compare canonical state before acting so scheduled workflows do not generate meaningless activity.

03

Explicit errors

Failures should route somewhere visible, with enough context to understand what happened.

04

Human checkpoints

Automation should stop for approval when the cost of a wrong action is higher than the cost of a review.

05

Traceability

Inputs, decisions and outputs should be inspectable without dumping sensitive data into logs.
